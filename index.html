What is VAT_Baker?

VAT_Baker is a professional-grade Vertex Animation Textures system designed for Unity developers who demand performance.

Convert traditional bone-based animations into GPU-friendly textures. Each pixel Stores vertex positions and normals for every frame, enabling the GPU to animate meshes directly—bypassing the CPU entirely.

Whether you're building a mobile game with hundreds of units or a AAA project with massive crowds, VAT_Baker scales to meet your needs while maintaining silky-smooth framerates. Works with ECS/Burst or the new no-DOTS MonoBehaviour workflow, on URP and Built-in Render Pipeline.

New in v1.1: MonoBehaviour baking, real vertex-shader crossfades, Built-in Render Pipeline shader, automatic clip detection, and correct bake for scaled/non-uniform hierarchies.
